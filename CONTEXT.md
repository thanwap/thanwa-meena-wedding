# Thanwa & Meena wedding

The wedding website for Thanwa & Meena (26 December 2026, Pattaya): invitation, RSVP, guestbook, photos and wedding gifts.

## Language

### Wedding gifts

**Gift transfer** (โอนซอง):
Money a guest sends to the couple through the PromptPay QR on the site instead of a physical envelope.
_Avoid_: Payment, donation

**Transfer notice** (แจ้งการโอน):
A guest's own record of a Gift transfer: who it is from and when it was sent, optionally with the amount and the slip.
_Avoid_: Payment confirmation, receipt

**Giver** (ผู้ให้):
The person or people a Gift transfer is from, in their own words — one name or several ("ครอบครัวพัวมณี", "ต้อม & แนน").
_Avoid_: Sender, payer

**Account holder** (เจ้าของบัญชี):
The bank account a Gift transfer left from, as shown (often partly hidden) in the bank app and on the slip. Not necessarily the Giver.
_Avoid_: Sender name

**Verified** (ตรวจแล้ว):
A Transfer notice the couple has matched to a real incoming transfer in their bank app. Its amount is then the bank's amount, not the guest's.
_Avoid_: Confirmed, approved

**Slip** (สลิป):
The bank app's receipt image for a Gift transfer. Seen only by the couple, and deleted by them once they no longer need it; the rest of the Transfer notice stays.
